Node types — ICIJ Offshore Leaks
Entity
A company, trust or fund created in a low-tax, offshore jurisdiction by an agent.
Officer
A person or company who plays a role in an offshore entity — e.g. a director, shareholder or beneficiary.
Intermediary
A go-between who helps set up offshore entities — often a law firm or middleman that liaises with the offshore provider.
